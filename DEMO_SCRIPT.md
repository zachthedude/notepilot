# NoticePilot screen-recording script

Target length: about 90 seconds. Record at 1440x900 (or full-screen 1080p) in a fresh browser window with no other tabs.

## Before you record

- Open https://zachthedude.github.io/notepilot/ and scroll back to the top.
- Close notifications. Set browser zoom to 100%.
- Clear the GPA, Age and Programme level fields if you've used them (reload the page).
- Practise the scroll once so the feature explorer moves at a steady pace.

## Script

| Time | On screen | What to do | What to say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Hero with the paper-plane logo | Hold still, then move the mouse over the hero so the plane tilts. | "Scholarship and exam notices bury deadlines, fees and rules in dense text. NoticePilot reads one for you, and shows the exact sentence behind everything it finds." |
| 0:10–0:15 | Hero cards | Point at "Rule-based, not AI". | "It's rule-based and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Feature explorer ("Built for your ...") | Scroll slowly through all six chips: Notice, Requirements, Sources, Conflicts, Eligibility, Checklist. Pause about 3 seconds on each. | Notice: "Paste any notice." Requirements: "It marks deadlines, fees, documents, rules and contacts." Sources: "Every item keeps its source line; if it can't quote it, it doesn't show it." Conflicts: "This notice gives two different deadlines, so it flags the conflict instead of picking one." Eligibility: "Answer up to three questions and each rule becomes match, no match or unknown." Checklist: "And you get an ordered checklist." |
| 0:40–0:45 | Top bar | Click **Take the tour**. | "There's a guided tour for first-time users." |
| 0:45–1:05 | Tour stops 1 to 4 | Click **Next** through stops 1 to 3. On stop 4 click **Load it for me**, then **Next**. | "The tour walks you through each control. I'll load the sample notice." |
| 1:05–1:10 | Tour stops 5 to 8 | Click **Finish** quickly or press Esc. | (no line needed) |
| 1:10–1:20 | Demo: Your details | Type GPA `3.7`, Age `24`, level `undergraduate`. Scroll to the results. | "As I answer, each rule updates to match, and every result shows the quote and line number it came from." |
| 1:20–1:25 | Conflicts panel | Point at the two dates. | "Here's the conflict: the 15th and the 20th of December. You're told to confirm with the issuer." |
| 1:25–1:30 | Checklist | Click **Copy checklist** so "Copied." appears. | "Copy the checklist and you're done. Every line traces back to the notice." |

## Closing line (optional, over the hero or the GitHub repo)

"NoticePilot: proof-carrying actions for public notices. Open source, free, and nothing leaves your browser."

## Things not to say

- Don't call it AI-powered; it's rule-based.
- Don't say it decides official eligibility; it compares your answers to the notice's own rules.
- Don't say it works on every notice format; it's tuned for the scholarship sample format today.
- Only mention Devin if the Devin adapter PR actually exists, and then say Devin contributed a tested adapter, not that the app calls Devin.
