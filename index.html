Checklist for GPA 3.7, age 24, undergraduate

    Paste any public notice.

    It stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server or saved.

    It finds what the notice asks of you.

    Deadlines, fees, required documents, eligibility rules and contacts, each one marked where it appears.

    Every item keeps its exact source.

    The sentence it came from sits beside it, with the line number. If an item can't be quoted, it isn't shown.

    Two dates for one deadline?

    NoticePilot flags the conflict and tells you to confirm with the issuer. It never picks one for you.

    Answer up to three questions.

    Each rule comes back as match, no match or unknown. A question you skip stays unknown.

    Copy your checklist.

    Conflicts first, then deadlines, documents, fees and contacts, ready to paste into your notes.
